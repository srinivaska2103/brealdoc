Common Brake Warning Signs

Squeaking or Grinding Noises

High-pitched squeals or structural metallic scratching indicate completely exhausted friction pads.

Steering Wheel Vibration

Pulsations under application hint at warped steel discs experiencing major thermal thickness variations.

Longer Stopping Distances

Delayed vehicle response parameters denote heavy component glazing or degraded hydraulic line pressure.

Brake Warning Dashboard Light

Illuminated console icons flag critical low reservoir fluid limits or active anti-lock circuit telemetry exceptions.

Vehicle Pulling Side-to-Side

Lateral drifts trace directly back to unaligned caliper slide pins or sticking mechanical piston.

Soft or Spongy Brake Pedal

A mushy lever threshold confirms structural master cylinder air leaks or heavily contaminated old fluid.
